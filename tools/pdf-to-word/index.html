Everything You Need to Know About PDF to Word Conversion

PDF documents preserve visual design across platforms, but making structural changes or editing long passages can be challenging. Converting PDF files into editable Word documents allows you to easily update text, adjust formatting, and repurpose content.

How Browser-Based Conversion Works

PDF files rely on fixed coordinate layouts, whereas Word documents use flowing text, paragraphs, and styles. Flipp extracts text items, inspects font parameters, groups adjacent lines into paragraphs, detects headings, and maps them into real Microsoft Word OOXML elements entirely inside your browser.

Complete Document Privacy

Unlike traditional conversion websites that require uploading confidential reports, financial statements, or legal contracts to third-party cloud servers, Flipp processes everything on your device. Your document contents remain completely private.
