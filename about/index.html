About Flipp

We built Flipp because the standard online PDF tools were fundamentally broken. When you needed to merge PDF files containing sensitive business contracts, or split PDF documents with personal medical records, traditional services asked you to compromise. They required you to upload your private files to distant servers, putting your data at risk while you waited in artificial queues, often to be met with a paywall or an account creation screen.

We envisioned a better way. Flipp exists to provide a privacy-first online PDF toolkit that never compromises on security, speed, or accessibility. Our core belief is simple: your documents belong to you, and they should never leave your device.

How Client-Side PDF Processing Works

Flipp is a true browser PDF editor. When you use Flipp to compress PDF files or convert documents, the entire operation happens locally within your web browser.

We don't have servers that process your files. We don't have databases storing your documents. Instead, Flipp downloads the necessary processing logic to your device when you load the page. Your computer's processor does all the work directly in the browser memory. Once you refresh or close the tab, the data is gone forever. This client-side PDF processing guarantees absolute privacy, making Flipp one of the most secure PDF tools available.

Who is Flipp Built For?

Flipp is designed for anyone who works with documents and values their privacy:

  • Professionals & Businesses: Handle confidential contracts, financial statements, and NDAs without violating company data compliance policies.
  • Students & Teachers: Quickly combine assignments, extract text for notes, and manage educational materials for free.
  • Everyday Users: Anyone needing a quick, reliable PDF converter without the hassle of signing up for an account.

Our Core Philosophy

Privacy First

Files never touch our servers. Zero uploads. Zero data collection. What happens on your device stays on your device.

Fast Performance

No waiting for uploads or server processing queues. Conversions happen instantly, powered entirely by your device's hardware.

Free & Accessible

Premium tools shouldn't require premium subscriptions. Flipp is free to use, requiring no account creation or credit card.

Simplicity

A clean, intuitive interface free of clutter. You drag, you drop, you get your results immediately. No complex menus or hidden settings.

Powered by Open Source

Flipp stands on the shoulders of giants. Our privacy-first PDF tools are made possible by an incredible ecosystem of open-source technologies. The engine behind our fast and secure processing includes trusted, industry-standard libraries like PDF.js for parsing, pdf-lib for complex manipulation, jsPDF for generation, JSZip for file bundling, and Tesseract.js for powerful client-side optical character recognition (OCR).

By leveraging modern JavaScript (ES Modules) and these robust libraries, Flipp delivers a desktop-class editing experience directly in your web browser.
